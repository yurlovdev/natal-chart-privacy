# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 28, 2026_

Natal Chart – Placidus respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you use the app.

## Information Collection and Use

The app does not require account registration and does not collect account information such as your email address, contacts, or password.

Birth data, including date, time, and place of birth, is used to calculate natal charts and compatibility-related information. Profiles and birth data are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to external servers for chart calculation or profile storage.

The app does not provide cloud sync or cloud backup for profiles. If you delete the app, erase app data, or change devices, locally stored profiles may be lost.

## Location

If you grant location access, the app may use your device’s precise location to help you find your position on the map while selecting a place.

When you submit a crash report or feedback from within the app, the app may include your current coordinates with the report to help diagnose issues. This location data is used only for support and debugging, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is not shared with data brokers.

## In-App Purchases

The app may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

The app does not collect or store your payment card information. Apple may provide the app with transaction information needed to unlock purchased features, restore purchases, and maintain purchase state.

Restoring a purchase restores access to the purchased feature. It does not restore locally stored profile data that may have been deleted from your device.

## Analytics and Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase services provided by Google to improve stability and performance:

- **Firebase Crashlytics** — collects crash reports, stack traces, and diagnostic information such as device type, operating system version, and app state at the time of a crash.
- **Firebase Analytics** — collects usage data and interaction events to help understand how the app is used and to improve functionality.

This data is used for app functionality, analytics, debugging, and stability improvements. It is not used by the app for advertising or tracking purposes.

You can learn more about Google’s data practices here:  
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## User-Submitted Information

The app provides an optional feedback and crash reporting feature that allows users to voluntarily submit information to help improve the app.

Any information submitted through this feature is sent to **Firebase Firestore** and may include user-entered text, technical details related to the app state, and, if available, location coordinates.

This information is used solely for support, debugging, and improving the app. It is not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

## Data Sharing

The app does not sell or trade your personal data.

The app may share limited data with service providers used to operate and improve the app, including Firebase services provided by Google and Apple services used for App Store purchases. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

## Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
